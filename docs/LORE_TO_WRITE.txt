HOIST: THE LORE THAT IS STILL TO BE WRITTEN
Written 2026-11-19 for the owner. Plain text. Each item says what it is, where it shows in the game, what is there now, and what is
needed from you. Write your answer under "yours:" and I carry it into the game (and into docs/CANON.md if it is a decision).
Nothing here is ever said to the player in these words.

The rules every line keeps (docs/RULES.md 1-11):
  - Horror is implied, never explained. A wrong detail, an ordinary reading for it, a shrug. Two to four short sentences.
  - Bright reading first: everything strange is first something cosy.
  - Never in player text: lid, eye, sleeper, Lady, "kept soft", "Mara is from". Before the hut is known: no "she", "the hut" or a
    number about it. ("The Eye" may be named only at the very bottom of the dread.)
  - Mara never explains and never confirms. One or two short lines. Calls you Captain.
  - One idea per line. No glyphs, no emoji. The village's name is never written anywhere.
  - Only the tune ending is happy.

and it never explains. The game never ties two pieces together (owner 2026-11-19): the player does that.
What each question still lacks (after R.'s letters):
  Q1 Who counts         fine (Crown, Lantern, Free Cays). More would help in Reef or Home water.
  Q2 What is under it   fine (Free Cays, Lantern, Drowned). A Crown or Reef piece would give a fourth.
  Q3 Who sat before you strong everywhere.
  Q4 What a throw costs fine (Home, Reef, Crown, Free Cays).
  Q5 Why Tama is home   still the thinnest: Home and Reef, plus the bell thread when it wakes. Needs one more in Crown, Free Cays
                        or Drowned water.
  Q6 What Mara is       Home, Drowned, the bell thread. Mara stays a mystery forever (CANON 2026-11-18): any piece must stay oblique.
  Q7 Can it end         Free Cays, Lantern, Crown. One in Reef or Drowned would help.
Kinds you can write (one line of idea is enough; I build it):
  - an EVENT at sea: something seen once, under a condition (a tide, a night, a storm).
  - a RIDDLE that is a place: a description to match, solved only by being there at the right time.
  - a LETTER: more of R., or someone new.
  - a REMNANT: an object that goes into the hold with a name and no explanation; whoever buys it says one plain thing.
  - an ECHO: the same thing seen in two waters (like the second cup on the customs desk and the cup in the Cays).
    yours:
