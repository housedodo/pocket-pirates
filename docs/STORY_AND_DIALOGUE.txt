HOIST (Pocket Pirates): STORY AND ALL DIALOGUE
================================================
Working file to edit and expand. Part 1 is the story as it stands (built + planned). Part 2 is every line the
game currently says, pulled straight from the code and grouped by source file and data table. Tags like [intro],
[outro], [hints], [once] are the field each line sits in. ${x} is a value filled in by the game.
Dread stages: 0 Sunny, 1 Off, 2 Wrong, 3 Eerie, 4 Cosmic (by distance from Tama, not by time).
Rule of the world: horror is implied, never explained. The captain never speaks. The Lady is never seen.


PART 1: STORY
=============

THE PREMISE
- You captain the Merry Gull, a small cheerful sloop, from Harbour Tama with Mara, your first mate.
- The sea is a closed eyelid. Dread is the Eye stirring in its sleep; the worst effects are its dreams leaking.
- A Lady in a black hut keeps it asleep by throwing a d20. Each low roll is a moment it nearly woke; she writes it
  in a ledger. The player's throws count too, without them knowing.

CHAPTERS (built, about one hour; this is the demo)
  I    Sea legs          0:00  sail, learn the wind ring, dock at Tama. Perrin has left a letter.
  II   Perrin's riddle   0:08  the riddle of Hollow Cay Galen: find the right shore, dig. First treasure (120 gold).
                               Buy an upgrade. Wind dies: oars and fishing. Wind returns: tack upwind.
  III  Earn your keep    0:18  a delivery, a passenger, chart 8 islands, the tavern (crew or dice).
  IV   Check my chart    0:30  Perrin's note names a far island and a detail; sail there and look.
  V    Strange notes     0:42  two bottles speak of a black hut; tavern gossip elsewhere confirms it.
                               The hut appears on the chart by itself. Mara asks who drew it.
  VI   The table         0:52  first throw at the hut. End card "TO BE CONTINUED"; "Keep sailing" lifts the fog.
  The fog wall widens per chapter (430, 430, 820, 1250, 1650, 1650 units from Tama).

HAND-MADE PLACES (built)
- The Grey Tooth (crag): cairn, tin box with a ship list, KESTREL crossed out; drawing of a white post. Charts Ribs, Bell.
- The Drowned Bell: a leaning tower over a drowned street; the bell has no clapper; a light in a window later.
- Hollowgreen (forest): water, timber, fruit; repeat visits heal.
- Wick's Rock: Wick the silent keeper; reveals half the sea on your chart.
- The Ribs: ship graveyard behind breakers (needs a stronger hull); salvage every few days.
- Glass Lagoon: inside a reef (needs shallow draft); a chest on the bottom.
- The Halved Isle: inside a fog bank (needs a lantern); half a house, half a strongbox.

WORLD HINTS (built, rare): the Kestrel's transits with daymarks, tide wrecks at low water, abandoned camps,
watchers on shores, birds that point the way, the arch's noon shadow over a buried chest.

ENDINGS (decided, not built): the final throw of the Lady's d20, plus a hidden ledger bonus/penalty from all
your earlier throws, revealed only at the end:
  - The Long Sleep (20): she sleeps a hundred more years.
  - Good Dreams (10-19).
  - She Rises (1-9).
  - Take Her Seat: you sit at the table (needs 3 offerings and 3 strange rumours followed); the hut is now on Tama.
  - The Unseen Throw: you leave (only for those who stopped throwing after the first).
  Full dread (built): leviathan arches, then "the Eye awakens"; you wake at Tama.

OPEN THREADS (started, not resolved)
- The Lady in the Hut: every throw is recorded. Ideas: the hut changes on later visits (more candles, a second
  chair, a ledger page with your ship drawn on it); a natural 1 leaves the hut empty; trinkets from high rolls
  (a tooth with a tiny ship carved in it, a warm black pearl) return as keys or offerings.
- The Eye: star-eye in the sky at full dread; to be tied to the ledger.
- The Pained Raven (working name): the Gull's opposite, a ghost ship. Seed: inside the hull, "Not the Gull. I left it."
- Mara: at full dread she forgets her village and says things she should not. What is she? Does she remember?
- Old Perrin: never met. His riddles describe shores he could not have seen; maps drawn as if from below.
- The Kestrel: crossed out of the Tooth's ship list. Its daymarks still stand.

SIDE STORY SEEDS (ideas)
 1 The Keepers' Ledger: lighthouse logs list ships not yet sailed; yours, dated next week.
 2 Perrin, the cartographer who never left port; he once visited the hut.
 3 Mara's village: on no map, because it was above water before the sea rose.
 4 The drowned bell: bring clappers from five wrecks; ringing it calls something up for one night.
 5 The fisherman's debt: he drags you home after sinking and keeps asking to be paid. Never in gold.
 6 Letters from a captain on your route; the handwriting slowly becomes yours.
 7 The twin harbour: two of the same name; the people in one do not blink.
 8 Lantern Guild oil comes from a deep fish; by its light the world shows what it hides.
 9 The raider who surrenders and warns that the sea further out is eating sectors.
10 The other dice: wrecks hold d20s each missing a number. The Lady wants them back.
11 The choir of gulls: at high dread their cries spell the hut's location. She keeps one very old gull.
12 The island that moves: in a new place each time it is charted. It is resting on something.
Little harbour quests: the lost kitten; the birthday cake; the other kitten (the same quest, slightly wrong).

ACT IDEA FOR THE FULL GAME
 1 sunny sailing and goals  2 rumours of The Drowned  3 the Lady's errands (a gull feather, a bell clapper,
 Mara's hat)  4 the descent through Eerie and Cosmic waters  5 the ending at the Eye.
