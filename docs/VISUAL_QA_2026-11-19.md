# Visual QA pass, 2026-11-19

Harness: `tools/visualqa/capture.cjs` (screenshots plus telemetry per shot) and `tools/visualqa/sheet.py` (contact sheets), test tooling only.
Four Sonnet workers captured about 145 frames: zones and landmarks (42), the state matrix of time x weather x dread with horizon views (35),
draw distance, elevation and glitch probes (48), and before/after re-captures of every fix (two rounds). Software rendering (swiftshader):
fps numbers are only relative; feel and timing are not judged. No console or shader errors in any frame.

## Fixed (verified by re-capture, before and after)

## Found, not fixed (owner's call or polish)
- Polish: at night the fully fogged far sea is a flat grey band between the navy sky and the black near sea (hor_*_t95). It also makes island
  silhouettes readable at night. A fix would bring `stage.fog` closer to `stage.skyHorizon` at night (daynight.js).
- Polish: a faint pale streak along the horizon from very high cameras after the edge fix (eld_120); no seam.
- Minor, design: the belt's teeth stay dark; small white squares on the sea near it are the sea's own foam pattern.
- Not defects (checked): the Halved Isle hidden in its fog bank (the barrier, by design); a red frame in deep fog (the damage flash: that spot
  is on the belt at high water); orange and sand skies far out at noon (the dread palette); the blocky water patches (deep and shallow).
- Harness limits: toasts are outside #hud, so `hud: false` does not hide them; a dev server reloads on source edits mid-run.
