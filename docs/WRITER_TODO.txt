1.4  PERRIN'S LETTERS AFTER THE HUT (objectives.js, perrin_*; full game only)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
After the first throw, Perrin sends the captain into each region in turn. A letter never says what to find; it is
done when the captain has seen anything that region holds. Each has: the letter, Mara's intro, and her hint when
you are stuck. (Mara's first intro line is the same for all five: "A letter from Perrin came in with the post, Captain.")

  Crown Traders' waters
     OBJ-0152  The Crown writes down every ship that comes in. Ask to see the book. Tell me whose name is at the top.
     OBJ-0155  (hint) Perrin wants us in the Crown Traders' waters. The chart shows whose waters we are in.
     yours:

  Free Cays
     OBJ-0156  In the Free Cays they sleep by day and play by night. Go at night. Take nothing but your ears.
     OBJ-0158  (hint) Perrin wants us in the Free Cays. The chart shows whose waters we are in.
     yours:

  Lantern Guild's waters
     OBJ-0159  The Guild's lamps burn a colour I have no name for. You will tell me I am wrong. Go and look.
     OBJ-0161  (hint) Perrin wants us in the Lantern Guild's waters. The chart shows whose waters we are in.
     yours:

  Reef Brotherhood's waters
     OBJ-0162  The Reef counts its wrecks. Count them yourself. I would like a second opinion.
     OBJ-0164  (hint) Perrin wants us in the Reef Brotherhood's waters. The chart shows whose waters we are in.
     yours:

  The Drowned waters
     OBJ-0165  The Drowned waters have streets, and streets have doors. Go at low water, and count the doors.
     OBJ-0167  (hint) Perrin wants us in the Drowned waters. The chart shows whose waters we are in.
     yours:

  Mara at the edge of the sea, after the hut (where the ship needs better parts):
     OBJ-0168  The shipwrights say a stronger hull or better sails go further than this, and on a falling tide. Past that, nobody sells charts.
     yours:


1.5  THE THREAD CLUES (clues.js, CL-T-*): the line added to the log the first time each thread is met
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A clue line is a fact in another hand that never concludes. Before the hut is known it may not say she, her, the hut,
or a number.
     CL-T-music    (Free Cays)  A tune that never ends. Nobody near it sleeps without it.
     CL-T-bell     (Lantern)    A bell under the water rings the hours of Tama, not of this place.     (feeds "Why is Tama home")
     CL-T-coin     (Crown)      A coin that finds its way back to the purse it left.
     CL-T-house    (Crown)      A house that keeps a book of who stayed. Our name is in it.
     CL-T-captain  (Reef)       A ship like ours in the wrong colours, lower in the water.
     CL-T-colour   (Drowned)    A patch of sea the wrong colour. The fish from it taste of nothing.
     CL-T-hollow   (Drowned)    A harbour that knew we were coming, and had a place set.
     yours:
