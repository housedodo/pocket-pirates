Fishing: where do I find what

In the demo only six bright species ever bite, and they bite everywhere, so there is nothing to look for. Each design below gives every fish a place and a time, and a way for the player to learn it. All three can be combined: A is the record, B the clue at sea, C the clue ashore.

A · The fisherman's log. Journal page with one row per species: where it lives (shallows, reef edge, open blue, deep water, wrecks, atoll lagoon, mangrove, harbour), the time of day, and your own best catch. A row shows the place once you have caught one or heard about it; until then a "? ? ?" row hints at the kind of water. The fish themselves are rules, not new art: a species is picked by what is near the boat (island type, distance from land, depth, hour, tide).

B · Spots at sea. You can see where to fish: a bubbling or glittering patch (silver for shoals, dark for deep water, pale for a reef edge), each with a tag when you slow down near it. A fish you never found by chance becomes findable by eye. Spots you have fished are drawn on the chart as a small fish, so you can come back.

C · Harbour talk. Villagers, fishers and the tavern tell you where one species bites (one hint per conversation), and it goes into the log right away. Gives the fishermen of every harbour a reason to be talked to, and lets a player who does not search the sea still learn the waters.
