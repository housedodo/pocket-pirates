Docking: three layouts

The problem: the harbour menu covers the messages (quest done, rumour bought) and mixes everything in three tabs. Each layout below gives every place in the village its own entry, so you pick what you came for, and gives the messages a spot that no menu ever covers. The villager's line (when there is one) still comes first, as now.

A · Signposts on the pier. Five wooden signs along the bottom: Board, Market, Tavern, Shipwright, Set sail (keys 1-4, E). A red number on a sign means something waits there (two errands to finish). The chosen place opens as one panel on the right; messages stack on the left, under the harbour name, where no panel ever goes. The harbour and the ship stay visible in the middle.

B · The harbour ledger. One wide, low book: the places on the left with a short line of what is there (the tavern says when rooms open, the market how much fish you carry), the chosen place on the right. What just happened is written at the foot of the page, inside the book, so it can never be hidden behind it. The most orderly of the three: everything at a glance, arrow keys up/down.

C · Walk the village. No menu on arrival: the camera rests on the village and each place gets a little pixel pin over its own building. Pick one (arrows or click) and only that place's card opens. Messages come up at the bottom middle, above everything. The most atmospheric (you see the village change at night and with dread) but it needs a camera framing per harbour, so it is the most work.
