Shipyard: preview before you buy

Today the yard is a long list that covers half the screen, and tapping a swatch buys it at once. All three options below let you try anything first (owned or not), look at the ship from every side, and only then pay. Nothing is paid for until you press Buy.

A · The turntable. The ship floats alone on calm water, no dock. Drag to spin it a full 360 degrees (or the arrows turn it in quarter steps), scroll to zoom in on the figurehead or the stern. The panel on the right is short: four tabs, one row of swatches. Choosing a swatch repaints the ship at once, marks it "TRYING" with its price, and Buy or Put back finishes it. The cleanest and the most like a shop window.

B · The fitting room. Everything on one low strip at the bottom, the ship large above it, slowly turning by itself (click and drag to take over). You change several things, see the sum of what is new, and buy it in one go or undo it. Fewer clicks for a full makeover, and the whole outfit is visible together.

C · The lookbook. Six ready-made looks tied to the factions (hull, sails, pennant and figurehead chosen together), shown as turning thumbnails; click one to see it full size on the turntable. Good for players who do not want to pick four things; each preset can be fine-tuned afterwards. The most work to build, and the best for showing the factions' identities.
