## 2026-11-18 The end of HOIST: the arrival and what each ending does (owner's decisions, CANON 2026-11-18; branch wt-endings)
- **Arriving at the Table is a ritual.** Within 340 units the six candles on the table light one by one as the ship comes in, the fog
  draws back, the sound thins (`audio.hush` up to 0.75) and Mara stops remarking. Someone sits in the far chair, in the 3D world and in
  the painted card: the first time she is seen. The card text is new (`ARRIVAL`, a draft); the choices are as they were. On going
  ashore the voyage is copied aside (localStorage `pocket-pirates-table-v1`).
- **A drawing lies on the table** (3D and card): a ship going down a hole if the Edge was gone over, else the Edge alone and "what is
  down there? who is there? why is it there?" in a crude red hand.
- **Good Dreams** changes nothing: the voyage goes on where it was, dread and all (it used to set the ship off Tama with dread cleared).
- **She Rises is the true game over.** The Eye's sequence plays to the white and nobody wakes; a still of the lit sea, only the die on
  the sand; then **Begin again** or **Go back to the table**. The title shows the same two. Gone back, Mara says once, quietly, that she
  is afraid it will not come out above nine, and the die shows the same face as before.
- **The Tune Is Kept** opens an errand: "Something left for you", Tama's quay. Docking there, a bundle in old sailcloth, tied with
  fishing line, no note; Mara: "I did say one of us ought to learn." Aboard, at night it plays wherever the ship is (placeholder: the
  Free Cays night guitar, `fc_night`, guitar alone) and the night keeps no dread. It lies by the stern rail, with a lamp and a small sway
  while it plays (the sound's silent twin).
- **The Unseen Throw**: four minutes of ordinary sailing, then for two and a half the world turns wherever the ship is: no new sails,
  Tama's bell stops, the blank line in the Ship's book is filled "in a small neat hand" with a number, Mara asks where everybody went.
  Then a table stands on the water ahead, a candle lit, someone in the far chair, the near chair pulled out. It stays ahead. Reaching it
  ends the voyage like She Rises.
- **Your Seat** ends in an epilogue: the book, open, once (pages of rows, tallies and little ships, turning faster, nothing readable),
  then the hut from the far chair: a ship with green sails, someone in the doorway, the hut's first words from your side; the newcomer
  is new, bright and anxious. Credits, then the title (Begin again / Go back to the table).
- **The Long Sleep** is unchanged (open: the owner decides).
- **Mara stays a mystery**: Your Seat's line "Across the table Mara sits down, slowly. She does not ask." (it put her in the newcomer's
  chair and read as knowing) is now "Down at the boat, Mara has lit the lamp. She does not call up." A draft like the rest.
- Every new line is a draft for the writer: `docs/WRITER_TODO.txt` 4.5. Not in the demo (RULES 29). Old saves load (new state is
  `story.over`, `story.again`, `story.againSaid`, `story.guitar`, `story.unseen`, all absent by default).
- Open for the owner: whether Your Seat should also offer "Go back to the table" (built: it does, like the other closed endings);
  the guitar's own tune; the number in the Unseen Throw's book line (built: any of 1 to 9, at random); the credits.
