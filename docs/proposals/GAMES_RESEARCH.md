# What HOIST can take from Pirates!, Port Royale and their kin (2026-11-25)

Proposal, nothing built. Two research passes (`docs/proposals/research/research_pirates.md`: Pirates! 1987/2004, Sea Dogs/Corsairs,
Black Flag, Windward; `research_portroyale.md`: Port Royale 1-4, Patrician, Uncharted Waters, Anno, Sunless Sea). Caveat: the web pages
themselves could not be fetched; facts come from search summaries (cited) and, where marked, the researchers' memory (unverified).

## Already in HOIST (the tradition's core, covered)
Wind that matters and the weather gauge; treasure maps in halves; reputation per harbour with refusal; boarding struck ships; the
shipwright; tool-gated places (spyglass, bell, boat); one raider at a time and parley (no piling on: Black Flag's worst criticism);
stores, crew mood, night nerves and shore rest (Sunless Sea's pressure); manual trade (Anno's route UI is the most criticised thing).

## Recommended (ranked by fit with HOIST's rules: wordless, cosy-to-dread, implied horror, procedural)
| # | Idea | From | Area | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Harbours show their state**: full or bare stalls, lit or dark windows, boats at the quay, washing out; a harbour you keep supplied grows (a stall, a house, a second pier boat), a neglected one thins. In the deep the growth runs backwards. | Port Royale, Anno | design + gameplay | M |
| 2 | **Harbour events seen from the sea**: a smoke plume (fire: they want timber), a yellow flag (fever: they want limes, nobody comes down), ships queued outside (blockade: a raider crew), bunting (a feast). | 3 | **Discovery reports**: an island, a wreck, a sketch in the creature log, carried to the Crown's customs house, paid once and written in their book (and later found already written, in your hand). | Uncharted Waters | gameplay | S |
| 4 | **Needs turn wrong in the deep**: far harbours stop wanting cloth and rum and ask for things you should not sell (a cup with your ship's name, a day of food you do not have, the blue salt back). | Port Royale needs + Sunless Sea | gameplay | S |

## Not recommended
- **An age clock and retirement** (Pirates!): CANON keeps dread by place, never time, and the endings are at the Table.
- **A sword duel and the governor's dance** (Pirates!): iconic but combat-and-skill sub-games; the captain never speaks, and they pull
  toward action. A wordless boarding moment could come later if wanted.
- **Harpoon whaling**: the whales are part of the creature log and the dread (the long shadow); hunting them breaks that.
- **Convoys, automated routes, a pirate-threat setting**: management layers that HOIST does not need.

## Design rules taken from the criticism
Keep the first hour quick and rewarding (Port Royale's slow start); keep every value visible where it is decided (Anno); mistakes must be
recoverable (Patrician); no text-only aimless quests (Sea Dogs): every job names a place you can see.

## Second pass, from real sources (2026-11-25)
Sources this time: the full Pirates! (1987) manual and the Uncharted Waters: New Horizons manual (Internet Archive), the Anno 1503
and 1701 DS manuals, and Wikipedia's articles. Details and citations: `research/research_pirates_v2.md`, `research/research_portroyale_v2.md`.

**What the first pass got wrong.** Pirates! has no four-piece map; the dance is 2004 only. Uncharted Waters' three fames are trade,
piracy and adventure; it has crew *health* (scurvy after 50 days from port, rats, the cat and lime juice), not a mood gauge or mutiny;
discoveries are paid by rating, and differently by each buyer. No source has fire, plague or blockade events in Port Royale or
Patrician (only Anno has fire and disease): HOIST's harbour events are its own. Sunless Sea numbers stay unverified.

**Ranked for HOIST** (both researchers' ideas compared; fit with the rules first, then how much of HOIST it reuses)
| # | Idea | Source | Builds on | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | BUILT 2026-11-25: **The back door**: a harbour that refuses you can still be reached at night by the rowboat, at a beach or a water stair; you can sell to one buyer and hear the pier, but nobody signs on and the yard is shut. | Pirates! 1987 and UW, both | standing refusal, the boat | S-M |
| 2 | BUILT 2026-11-25: **Poor harbours ask no questions**: a thriving harbour keeps to the law (refuses sooner, wants papers); a dying one trades with anyone, and in the deep it is glad of you in a way that is not right. | Pirates! 1987 | plenty, standing | S |
| 3 | **News goes stale**: what you know of a harbour (its event, its plenty) is what you last saw or heard; the chart and the board show the old state until you go or a passing ship is hailed for news (masts, hull, colours as she comes up). | Pirates! 1987 | events, the chart, traffic | M |
| 4 | BUILT 2026-11-25: **The wanted good as a gap in the scenery**: an empty rope walk, a dry cask rack where the rum should be; a heap of crates on the pier where a good is plentiful. No price list needed to read it. | UW, Anno | trade WANTS, harbour layers | S-M |
| 5 | **Days from land**: after long weeks without a harbour the crew sickens (slower work, a hand lost); limes and fruit keep it off, a ship's cat keeps the rats from the stores. | UW | stores, mood, fever wants fruit | M |
| 6 | **Feed a village to be shown its find**: two casks of food to an out-island hamlet, and someone walks you to the one thing there; a hand may ask to stay. | UW | stores, islands, specialists | M |
| 7 | **Finds paid by how far**: the customs house pays more for what is far out, and the Guild and the Crown pay for different things. | UW | discovery reports | S |
| 8 | **Fresh catch and wrong catch**: fish sold the same day pay more; in deep water a fish comes up that the buyer will not touch, or pays too much for. | Dredge | fishing, dread | S |
| 9 | **What a prize costs**: keeping a captured ship takes eight of your men; sell, strip or burn her instead. | Pirates! 1987 | boarding | S |
| 10 | **A raider's cache**: dig up a crew's buried takings and that crew comes for you. | Pirates! 2004 | raiders, digging | S |
| 11 | **Three names, corrected**: trade, adventure (finds, charting) and piracy; the Crown's letter of marque is free once piracy leads. | UW | standing, raiders | M |

Set aside: the noon sight (an owner call: it is navigation by skill), a convoy with a route, Mara sailing painted lanes (trade is manual
by design), the 2004 dance, a wandering free trader (a maybe), a terror bar (HOIST keeps dread off the HUD), Dredge's panic by time
after dark (dread is by place).
