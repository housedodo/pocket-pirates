# Research: Pirates! and its tradition (for HOIST)

Method note: web search worked; page fetches (Wikipedia, GameSpot, PC Gamer) were blocked by the session network. "[S]" = stated in a search-result snippet with the URL given. "[M]" = from my own memory, NOT verified this session; treat as unconfirmed.

## Sid Meier's Pirates! (1987 and 2004)
- 2004 remake: Metacritic PC 88/100; Computer Games Magazine ranked it second-best PC game of 2004; Computer Gaming World called it "the year's most purely entertaining single-player game" [S] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sid_Meier's_Pirates!_(2004_video_game), https://www.metacritic.com/game/sid-meiers-pirates/
- Structure: a "sailing map" mode across the Caribbean, with sub-games for ship combat, boarding sword duels, courting governors' daughters by dance, searching for buried treasure, sneaking out of prison [S] https://giantbomb.com/wiki/Games/Sid_Meiers_Pirates_Live_the_Life
- Boarding a ship can trigger a captain-versus-captain duel; it is reflex and pattern based and gets easier once the patterns are learned [S] https://gamespot.com/sid-meiers-pirates/user-reviews/2200-85601/
- Goal: retire at a ripe old age with as much wealth and status as possible [S, product listing] https://www.ebay.com/p/208613003 . In the 1987 original the captain ages, fighting gets harder and failing health forces retirement; the 2004 version has fewer retirement jobs (Parson or Bishop is one) [S] https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/Pirates
- Freedom of style: IGN praised choosing your own style of play; 1UP noted the experience differs greatly between low and high difficulty [S] (Metacritic page above).
- Look: GameSpot noted cotton sails that glow softly in warm sun [S] https://gamespot.com/reviews/sid-meiers-pirates-review/1900-6114066/
- [M] Fame/score is a retirement tally of gold, land, titles, marriage, and a "fame" from deeds. Each nation's towns differ in architecture and flag; your standing with each of four powers (plus pirates) changes as you take their missions or raid them; crew shares and morale decide mutiny; ships are fast or slow by class and wind; treasure maps are in four fragments; the 2004 sword duel has a skill-adjusted opponent. None verified.
- Praise: pick-up-and-play loops, short varied minigames, a "choose your own life" sandbox. Criticism [M]: repetition in the late game, shallow economy, minigames outstaying their welcome.

## Sea Dogs / Corsairs / Age of Pirates (Akella)
- Three names for one series; Sea Dogs is widely seen as the first 3D pirate game, heavily inspired by Pirates! [S] https://gamesradar.com/a-23-year-old-cult-classic-pirate-series-has-been-revived-as-a-free-open-world-rpg-inspired-by-the-best-pirate-game-ever
- GamesRadar pros for Caribbean Tales: a faithful long-term crew, plundering ships and cargo, building pirate colonies. Cons: tedious combat, boring missions, thin story [S] https://gamesradar.com/age-of-pirates-caribbean-tales-review
- GameSpot: aimless, simple text-only quests; "drowns in the details" compared with Pirates! [S] https://gamespot.com/reviews/age-of-pirates-caribbean-tales-review/1900-6159850/ ; Corsairs 2000: cumbersome interface, lackluster presentation [S] https://gamespot.com/reviews/corsairs-review/1900-2538458/
- Fans: rewarding for those who like to think, historical flavour, free-play depth [S] GOG/Steam user reviews, https://www.gog.com/game/sea_dogs_caribbean_tales
- [M] Systems: RPG skills and perks, five-plus ship classes you buy or capture, nation relations with a separate pirate standing, officers you recruit with their own skills, on-foot town and fort assaults. Not verified.

## Assassin's Creed IV: Black Flag (and the 2026 Resynced remake)
- Naval combat is the standout; naval fights lead straight into boarding and swordfights; the crew sings dozens of collectible shanties; drum-heavy soundtrack in naval fights [S] https://www.tomsguide.com/us/assassins-creed-iv-review,review-1932.html , https://gamespot.com/reviews/assassin-s-creed-iv-black-flag-review/1900-6415509/
- Criticism: the ship "handles like a bus", wind direction has little meaning; battles can become impossible when reinforcements keep arriving; repetitive main missions, dated mechanics [S] https://www.pcgamer.com/au/assassins-creed-4-black-flag-review/ , https://www.theglobeandmail.com/technology/gaming/review-assassins-creed-iv-black-flag-a-jolly-pirates-life-for-you/article15131685/
- PC Gamer scored it 90, "gorgeous, relentlessly entertaining open-world piracy simulator" (same link).
- Resynced (Ubisoft, 26 May 2026): Kenway's Fleet fully integrated (ships sent on missions to gather, trade or fight; slots grow; ship class matters); captured ships can be used for repairs, to lower Wanted Level, or kept; diving bell needed for the deepest loot, free dives elsewhere; harpoon whaling with finite harpoons; hunting; two waterspout types; 35 original shanties found by exploring plus 10 new, with a shanty wheel; a pathfinder that pins a map destination [S] https://news.ubisoft.com/en-us/article/2ld4JIABg5aOvoiUlJlUWF/assassins-creed-black-flag-resynced-deep-dive-into-the-naval-gameplay
- [M] Island and harbour feel: Havana and Nassau as living towns with crowds and stalls; small hidden islands with treasure and ruins; sailing across a tropical sea with sea-and-sky mood changes; the Observatory; the Mayan temples. Not verified.

## Windward (Tasharen, 2015; sequel Windward Horizon 2025)
- Top-down, procedurally generated world with a ship; four starting factions mapped to exploration, combat, trade, diplomacy; factions expand and fight for port ownership; hidden Syndicate and Aequitas factions harder to join [S] https://www.softpedia.com/reviews/games/pc/Windward-Review-482194.shtml , https://store.steampowered.com/app/326410
- Talent points boost sailing speed, cannon accuracy and range, hull points [S] (same Steam page).
- GameSpot: the economy is comically simple yet drawn out (two-slot hold early); others call the sail-and-trade loop meditative [S] https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/windward-review/1900-6416142/ , https://kotaku.com/steams-latest-top-seller-is-a-cool-surprise-1704303702
- [M] Quests come from towns as short tasks, and the pleasant feel comes from slow exploration and reading the map.

## Candidate ideas for HOIST
1. Age as the clock: a voyage or "season" count that slowly limits what the captain can do (old hands tire, rope-work slows), ending in a quiet retirement option. Pirates! 1987. Fits the slow-turn dread: the clock is the horror, not a timer on screen. Check CANON first; the owner decides.
2. Retirement tally with no numbers: a last scene that shows what you kept (a house, a ship, a flag, a lantern) rather than a score. Pirates! 2004. Wordless HUD fits.
3. Boarding duel that carries the ship fight: a short reflex duel with the captain after you close alongside, with a pattern the player learns. Pirates!/Black Flag. HOIST has boarding struck ships; this adds a human face. Partly covered.
4. Skill-scaled duel: opponent reads the captain's age and crew mood. Pirates!. Cheap, uses existing mood.
5. Dance with a harbour notable (rhythm of two or three keys) to win a favour or a rumour. Pirates! 2004. Cosy early-game charm, sours in dread regions (the partner stops looking at you).
6. Town look by region: roofs, flag colours, lamp style, market stall goods differ by the five regions. Pirates! towns by nation [M]. HOIST has regions; make them readable from the sea at a distance. Likely partly covered.
7. Shanties as found objects: each is learned at a port, island or from a raider crew; the crew sings by mood and weather. Black Flag (35 shanties). Fits a procedural sound kit; in the dread, a shanty comes back wrong or one verse short.
8. Fleet by letter: send a captured or hired boat on a slow errand (trade, scout) and it returns, or does not. Black Flag Kenway's Fleet. Dread fit: one never returns. Needs owner check.
9. Captured ship choice: keep, strip for repairs, sell, or burn; strip may lower raider notoriety. Black Flag. HOIST has boarding; the choice is partly new.
10. Diving bell as depth gate: rarest loot only below a depth that needs the bell. Black Flag. Covered (diving bell exists).
11. Harpoon whaling or big-fish hunt with finite harpoons. Black Flag. Related to fishing, which HOIST has; a large-creature hunt could feed the creature log. New but off-theme risk.
12. Wind that matters, and shows: visible telltales on sails, pennants, gauge. Black Flag criticism ("wind has little meaning") is the opposite of HOIST. Covered (points of sail, weather gauge).
13. Treasure map in fragments: found in halves. Pirates!. Covered.
14. Reputation with refusal: harbours that will not sell to you. Pirates!/Sea Dogs. Covered (per harbour/region).
15. Crew as long-term companions: named hands who remember voyages and leave or stay by mood. Sea Dogs (loyal long-term crew praised). HOIST has hired hands and mood; named memory and a goodbye scene would be new.
16. Pathfinder replaced by a chart you draw: the captain's chart fills as you sail, with soundings and sketched coasts; no marker. Black Flag pathfinder (opposite) and Windward map-reading. HOIST rule fits: draw-your-own chart. Check whether the chart exists.
17. Procedural world seed per run so islands differ: Windward. HOIST already has fixed named wrecks; a seed is a big change, so only for minor islands.
18. Slow factions that move: ports change hands over weeks, so a regional power shifts between visits. Windward. Fits trade drift; dread version: a region's lamps go out.
19. Quiet loop on purpose: allow a meditative trade-and-sail mode without combat pressure. Windward (meditative loop praised). Covered by cosy start; keep it.
20. Gate loot by tool: spyglass, bell, boat unlock places rather than levels. Black Flag/Windward. Covered (shipwright, bell, boat).
21. Reinforcement caps: do not stack endless enemies in a fight (Black Flag criticism). A design rule: raiders break off or parley instead of piling on. Mostly covered by parley; add as a rule.
22. Skill levels as play style, not difficulty: let the player drop the duel or the combat to "story" (Pirates! praise for style choice). Check against RULES.
23. Task variety without text: tiny hand-made tasks from the board that need no quest marker (Sea Dogs criticism: text-only quests are aimless). HOIST board exists; use a place described by landmark.
24. Crew mood sung: a mood meter shown only as what the crew do (singing, silence, scrubbing). Black Flag shanties plus HOIST mood. Fits wordless HUD.

## What the tradition teaches
- Praised: short varied loops, naval combat fused to boarding, ports that feel inhabited, freedom to pick a life.
- Criticised: aimless text quests, cumbersome interfaces, vague wind, enemies that pile on, thin or drawn-out economies.
- HOIST already holds most of the systems; the largest gaps are age/retirement, a duel, dance, shanties, a fleet errand, and named crew memory. Source gaps: nation-by-nation town looks, 2004 fame formula and the Sea Dogs ship and reputation details are unverified.
